## A. Binding mission and interpretation

Build **MetriStay**, the hospitality suite from **Metrikingdom**, under the **MetriSys** technology brand: one integrated software platform for a hotel: PMS, direct and channel distribution, rooms/front desk, corporate bookings, function spaces, catering, bar/F&B, club, parking with AI camera or LPR server, engineering, utilities, gas, water, electricity, purchasing, inventory, HR/payroll, finance/AP/AR/GL, payment gateway, bill-provider interfaces, guest loyalty, management profitability, hotel-initiated guest travel and transport requests, and a searchable service-provider registry for every department, chef staffing continuity, a vendor mobile marketplace for verified hotel suppliers, source-to-pay purchasing, and low-touch receiving and consumption control. Provide web administration, guest web/mobile, staff mobile, and corporate web plus downloadable Android/iOS applications. Include property image/video publishing, locally deployable AI image enhancement, a guarded customer AI agent, identity-assisted check-in, electronic signatures, SMS/WhatsApp verification, incident response and lost-and-found in Release 1. Expand later into marketplace, multi-property, UC, HSIA, IPTV and advanced revenue AI.

**Jurisdiction classifier:** Configure country -> province/state/region -> municipality -> property legal entity -> transaction location/service/delivery context, with effective dates and evidence for tax, payroll, invoicing, guest registration, ID, privacy, payments, travel intermediation, permits and retention. Seed **Canada, Oman, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Portugal** in Phase 1. Treat rule sets as country-specific and validated, never as a globally uniform "compliance" toggle. Unknown, unvalidated or expired rule sets block automated filing, selling or payout that depends on them and show a manual/compliance review path. A jurisdiction can be planned and implemented behind a gate without falsely claiming government certification.

**Operating principle:** Start from a hotel's real decisions, not a menu of modules. For every role show "what needs attention, why, by when, who owns it, what can I do, and did it work?" Use progressive disclosure, task-based navigation, search, reusable forms, responsive offline-safe staff workflows, accessible guest journeys and explicit source/freshness on KPIs. A small hotel enables only the outlets, integrations and regulations it actually uses. A single-hotel Release 1 must run coherently with one property; broader chains and niche facilities are configurable or later. A required partner capability that cannot be certified is shown as a named external dependency and has an honest manual operating path. Never pretend that all countries have certified APIs or that every feature should appear in every user's navigation.

**One software** means a single property data and permission model, unified transaction IDs, coordinated workflows and consolidated reporting. It does not require reimplementing card networks, bank transfers, licensed wallets, utility-provider systems, camera analytics or payroll clearing. External partners remain system-of-record for their regulated service; our platform has certified adapters, visible statuses, reconciliation and recovery.

**Phase vs release:** Phase 1 is a full planning and architecture phase. Phases 2–6 are implementation phases and together form **Customer Release 1: Single Hotel**. No subset of these is represented as the completed first release. Phases 7–8 are later expansions. Internal slices can be demonstrated and pilot-tested while Release 1 remains incomplete.

**Architecture:** Preserve a viable existing codebase after inspection. If empty, choose a maintainable modular stack. Use one shared hotel domain, property-scoped authorization, append-only financial and stock ledgers, versioned contracts, transactional outbox/inbox, explicit adapter boundaries, localization (English/Arabic RTL), accessible responsive screens, SaaS and hotel on-premises deployment profiles, and documented backups/restore. Payments and money movements require separate authorization and audit. Keep offsite/offline operations resilient under stated constraints.

### Regulatory and partner facts to verify in Phase 1

- **Canada, Oman, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Portugal enter the Phase 1 jurisdiction classifier.** Maintain a dated, cited obligation inventory per country and relevant subnational location, hotel legal entity and offered service; separate confirmed requirements, assumptions, local-counsel questions, partner constraints and supported filing routes. Do not copy Canada or Oman rules to the other three.
- **Canada is a launch-market configuration, not an afterthought.** Identify province/territory and municipality for each property; version tax rules by jurisdiction, effective date, product and exemption. Canadian employment records use the **Social Insurance Number (SIN)**, with CPP/EI and income-tax deductions; Québec needs QPP and other Québec-specific rules. "NIS" is ambiguous here: capture its intended jurisdiction and meaning in Phase 1 and never treat it as a Canadian tax identifier by assumption. [CRA payroll deductions](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/calculating-deductions.html) • [CRA T4 guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/completing-filing-information-returns/t4-information-employers/t4-slip.html).
- Canadian hotel stays generally fall under GST/HST rules; determine provincial sales/accommodation and municipal lodging charges, place of supply and local exemptions with advisers for each location. Prepare compliant tax records, payroll remittance and permitted filings, but **do not assume a universal CRA/government API**: some submissions use certified software/file formats or portals. Validate each authorized interface and credential separately. [CRA travel industry GST/HST guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/gst-hst-information-travel-convention-industry.html) • [CRA GST/HST internet file transfer](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/software-gst-hst-internet-file-transfer.html).
- For ID images, facial/biometric processing, signatures and messaging, assess purpose, necessity, valid consent, provincial privacy obligations, retention and lawful alternatives in each market. Biometric match/liveness is optional and separately gated. A QR link alone is a handoff, not authentication. [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/health-information-genetics-biometrics/biometrics/bio-tips_org/) • [Identification and authentication](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/identities/identification-and-authentication/auth_061013/).

- Khedmah and ONEIC advertise electricity/water bill payment and other services. Their public consumer sites **do not establish that a merchant API is available**; obtain private API specifications, commercial terms, sandbox, authorized biller coverage and settlement rules before production integration. [Khedmah](https://khedmah.om/) • [ONEIC Pay](https://eservicesprd.oneic.ws/).
- A cash/stored-value wallet or money transfer may require licensing/regulated PSP arrangements. CBO publishes a PSP licensing policy; Phase 1 legal/payment workstream must establish the exact permitted model and provider obligations. [CBO PSP policy](https://cbo.gov.om/sites/assets/FintechCompulsoryDocs/Circular%20BM%201192-%20PSP%20policy.pdf).
- Oman has a wage-protection framework with a standardized salary information file. Verify current file format, bank workflow and employer obligations with the selected bank and Ministry before production. [Ministry of Labour](https://mol.gov.om/pages/ABOUT-WPS) • [WPS FAQ](https://www.mol.gov.om/FAQ).
- Oman Ministerial Decision 105/2021 prohibits sale/promotion through network or pyramid marketing. The Oman deployment must have **no active multi-level recruiting, downline commissions or pyramid payout flow**. Provide loyalty points for genuine purchases first. A one-tier booking referral is materially different from a recruiting chain, but its actual terms, advertising method, payer and recipient must be reviewed before activation. Use a non-hierarchical guest and commercial partner network. Any proposal for multi-level compensation in another market is outside this product plan until separate jurisdiction-specific legal advice and a new product decision. [Decision text](https://qanoon.om/p/2021/mociip20210105/).

### MetriStay Network — single-tier booking referral model

**Brand architecture:** **Metrikingdom** is the company; **MetriSys** is the technology brand; **MetriStay Hospitality Suite** is the hotel product. Its guest and referral ecosystem is **MetriStay Network** (external names require trademark, app-store and domain clearance). Guest loyalty: **MetriStay Rewards**; direct referrer dashboard: **MetriStay Partner Hub**; corporate portal: **MetriStay Business**.

**Exact commercial principle:** An enrolled referrer may receive **one contractual referral commission** only if a person directly referred by that referrer books a hotel or property through an attributable code/link, completes the paid stay, and the transaction is not reversed. The source of that commission is Metrikingdom's revenue/profit from **that booking**. The direct guest is free to book without joining the referral program. Referrers cannot recruit referrers for compensation, cannot earn from bookings generated by another referrer, and need not buy anything or meet recruitment quotas.

**Profit-share wording:** For legal and accounting clarity, draft the contract as a **commission measured by the realized contribution margin of the specific referred booking**, paid by the company as a marketing expense. Do not promise equity, dividends or a percentage of the entire company's net profit. Example only: net collected room revenue OMR 100 minus contractually defined attributable costs OMR 70 = eligible margin OMR 30; at a 20% commission rate the direct referrer earns OMR 6 after stay/settlement. Define allowed deductions (tax, refunds, OTA/gateway fees, variable fulfillment costs, any excluded services), negative/zero margin, multi-room bookings, cancellation, partial refund, calculation date and audit/dispute rights **before** displaying an earning estimate. The chosen percentage is a business decision, not a legal safe harbor.

| Party | What it can do | What it cannot earn from |
|---|---|---|
| Guest | Book a real hotel stay at published/contracted price; earn separate own-purchase loyalty points where eligible. | Becoming a referrer, joining a chain, or buying access to earnings. |
| Direct referrer | Share its unique link/code, disclose compensation, see directly attributed completed bookings and its commission statement. May be an individual or approved company under its applicable contract. | Recruitment of referrers; a referred guest's later referral activity; second/third-level bookings; team volume or ranks. |
| Hotel/company | Confirm reservation and payment, calculate contractually defined booking margin, approve commission, pay through authorized bank/PSP workflow and record tax. | Charging an entry fee to referrers or treating commission as guest-funded investment return. |

**Legal assessment gate:** Oman Decision 105/2021 defines banned network/pyramid marketing by a hierarchical/network grouping of recruited consumers in return for benefit and prohibits sales/promotion through it. The specified one-tier direct-sale commission **appears different from that model**, but this is an inference from the published text, not a definitive clearance. Counsel should assess the actual flow, including whether consumer referrers are organized as a marketing network, how it is promoted, whether a social-media marketing licence or promotional permit applies, whether acting as a travel agent triggers a tourism licence, and VAT/withholding/income treatment of recipients. The platform may implement and test this feature in Phase 5, but payment activation in Oman needs a documented legal and tax opinion for the final terms and referrer categories. In each other selling market, review its own consumer, advertising, tourism/agency, privacy, payment and tax rules; there is no single “international law” granting blanket permission. EU/Portugal pyramid rules focus on payments for recruitment rather than genuine sales, but advertising and data-protection obligations remain. US FTC guidance requires clear disclosure of compensated endorsements.

**Workflow and controls:**
1. Referrer signs an approved direct-referral agreement (no joining fee) and completes identity/tax/payment details. Approval is scoped to country, hotel legal entity and permitted promotional channels.
2. Referrer shares a unique code/link with a clear compensation disclosure. Guest clicks and books voluntarily; consent and attribution are recorded without exposing guest PII to the referrer.
3. Attribution engine awards **at most one direct referrer** to a booking with deterministic code/last-click/manual precedence, cookie and privacy policy, fraud/self-referral checks and a dispute queue. No recursive graph or upline/downline table exists.
4. After checkout, cleared payment and refund window, finance calculates booking-specific realized contribution margin and commission using the agreed cost version, then creates a pending commission ledger entry. Cancellation, chargeback, no-show or amended booking recalculates or reverses the entry.
5. Finance and compliance approve tax/document evidence and make a payout through an authorized payment channel. Referrer dashboard shows pending/approved/paid/reversed amounts and booking ID with privacy-preserving details. Guest price is unchanged unless a separately approved promotion applies.
6. Program admin can disable a country, hotel, referrer category or campaign instantly; server/API and background jobs enforce the same policy. Existing earned commissions remain governed by contract and are not silently deleted.

**Non-negotiable tests:** Two referrers claiming a booking result in one or disputed attribution, never double pay. A referrer recruiting another earns zero. A guest directly booked through A's code who later refers guest C pays A only for the first guest's eligible booking, never guest C. Refund reverses commission once. Booking with zero or negative eligible margin earns zero under the contract. A disabled jurisdiction rejects payout regardless of UI state. Audit shows booking, revenue, cost components, formula version, authorization and payment reference.

**Relevant primary guidance:** [Oman Ministry of Justice decision listing](https://mjla.gov.om/decisions/ar/1088/show/1454); [Oman social-media marketing licence](https://gov.om/en/w/get-a-license-to-practice-marketing-and-promotion-on-social-media-platforms); [Oman promotional permit](https://gov.om/en/w/get-a-permit-for-organizing-promotional-offers-discounts-and-marketing-cards); [Oman travel agency licence](https://gov.om/en/w/get-travel-and-tourism-agencies-license); [Oman tax withholding categories](https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/withholding-tax); [FTC affiliate disclosure](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking); [Portugal unfair-practices law](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/57-2008-246504).

## P. Simplicity, accessibility, data and release gates

1. **Product packaging:** Profile each hotel as limited/full service and activate only owned outlets. Role home screens show 3–7 highest-priority tasks with a unified inbox, contextual search, one authoritative status and progressive detail. Guest booking stays usable on low-bandwidth mobile and assisted front-desk channels. English/Arabic/RTL are initial languages; add jurisdiction-specific language packs in Phase 1 requirements. Use WCAG 2.2 AA as a design/test target, including keyboard, screen readers, error correction and accessible CAPTCHA-free alternatives. [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).
2. **Canonical commercial journey:** property media/SEO or distribution -> consented traffic attribution -> live search/quote -> policy and total cost -> inventory hold -> approved payment -> booking -> pre-arrival -> stay/ancillaries -> checkout -> survey/recovery -> consented return visit. Every state gets owner, timestamp, failure recovery and KPI; channel commissions and marketing spend are reconciled to realized contribution.
3. **Financial and stock invariants:** No ghost room sale, oversold timed space, double capture/refund/payout, duplicate stock receipt, pay-without-approved-evidence, stale vendor stock asserted as guaranteed, negative food lot, discarded item released for use, unconfirmed external travel marked booked, or unverified tax filing marked submitted. Corrections are reversing transactions with source links.
4. **Privacy/security:** Permission scopes at tenant/property/department/record/field; step-up auth for privileged actions; vendor/guest/corporate separation; defensible retention by country and record purpose; tokenized gateway payment and current PCI DSS applicability, never raw card storage in general PMS tables. Test API object authorization and tenant isolation against OWASP API risk categories. [PCI document library](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/document_library/) • [OWASP API Security](https://owasp.org/www-project-api-security/).
5. **Reality-based rollout:** Phase 1 estimates work by bounded team, cost, hardware, contracts and five-market compliance. Phases 2–6 deliver a coherent **single-property operating release**; specialist businesses (spa, golf, fleet, franchise) require full flows only when enabled for a pilot hotel, but must be specified and demonstrable with tests/fixtures. If the pilot hotel needs an external channel, PSP, payment biller, airline seller or government filing, that connector's real certification is a dependency, not a UI tick. Release 1 is blocked for a property when a required real workflow has only a mock. Later phases expand multi-property and OTA marketplace.
6. **Measurable exit:** Establish baselines and acceptance targets for guest quote-to-book conversion, direct net cost, occupancy/net RevPAR, complaints to closure, housekeeping turnaround, vendor on-time-in-full, food waste, average stock age, utility per occupied room, outstanding AR, payroll errors, payment disputes, incident acknowledgment, uptime and recovery. Targets are agreed with the pilot hotel in Phase 1, never fabricated as universally correct.
7. **Deliverable discipline:** Phase 1 produces the ten Section H documents plus `docs/10-competitive-gap-and-questions.md`, `docs/11-guest-acquisition-and-service.md`, `docs/12-operations-safety-continuity.md` and `docs/13-simple-experience-and-acceptance.md`. Every M01–M68 module must have features, numbered subfeatures, screen/API/data/exception/test and phase; every scenario in Section O must have a traceability row. Check for mismatches between Phase plan, catalogue, detailed breakdown, UI companion and acceptance suite before coding. Where business policy or local law is unknown, specify assumption, named owner, system gate and fallback; proceed with independent design work.
